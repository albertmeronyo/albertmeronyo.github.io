I am a Lecturer in Computer Science and Knowledge Engineering at King’s College London, United Kingdom. I am a member of the <a href="https://www.kcl.ac.uk/research/dai">Distributed Artificial Intelligence</a> group.

I obtained my PhD at the Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam in 2016, under the supervision of Frank van Harmelen, Stefan Schlobach, and Andrea Scharnhorst. I have done research at the Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences and the Autonomous University of Barcelona. My research focuses on Multimodal Knowledge Graphs, Web querying, and the intersections of AI, culture, and Digital Humanities.

Previously I was a postdoctoral researcher at the Knowledge Representation & Reasoning group, Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam, and at the International Institute of Social History. I have done work in various areas of the semantic web, most prominently in Web Data APIs over RDF Knowledge Graphs.
