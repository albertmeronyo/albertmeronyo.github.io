I am a Lecturer in Computer Science and Knowledge Engineering at King’s College London, United Kingdom. I am a member of the <a href="https://www.kcl.ac.uk/research/dai">Distributed Artificial Intelligence</a> group.

I obtained my PhD at the [Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam](https://vu.nl/nl) in 2016, under the supervision of [Frank van Harmelen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_van_Harmelen), [Stefan Schlobach](https://www.few.vu.nl/~schlobac/), and [Andrea Scharnhorst](https://pure.knaw.nl/portal/en/persons/andrea-scharnhorst/), as a [KNAW](https://www.knaw.nl/en) / [DANS](https://dans.knaw.nl/en/) funded student. I have done research at the [Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://www.knaw.nl/en) and the [Institute of Law and Technology, Autonomous University of Barcelona](https://webs.uab.cat/idt/). My research focuses on Multimodal Knowledge Graphs, Web querying, and the intersections of AI, culture, and Digital Humanities.

Previously I was a postdoctoral researcher at the [Knowledge Representation & Reasoning group](https://lr.cs.vu.nl/), Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam, and at the [International Institute of Social History](https://iisg.amsterdam/nl). I have done work in various areas of the semantic web, most prominently in Web Data APIs over RDF Knowledge Graphs.
