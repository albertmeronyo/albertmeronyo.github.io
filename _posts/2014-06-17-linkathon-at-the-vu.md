During last April 2, CEDAR participated in the great 1st VU Digital History Datathon (notice the difference with a hackathon). The goal of the day was to sit together with the many people applying Linked Data principles to historical datasets at the VU and its sorroundings, in order to:

- Get a clear **picture** of the different datasets, and the way they may be linked
- Produce as many **links** between these datasets as possible

[Victor de Boer](http://www.few.vu.nl/~vbr240/), with support from [Chris Dijkshoorn](http://www.few.vu.nl/~cdn370/about.html), [Niels Ockeloen](https://www.linkedin.com/in/nielsockeloen) and [Albert Meroño](https://www.albertmeronyo.org/), organized the day. Victor taught the audience on the [basics of RDF linking](http://www.slideshare.net/vdeboer/linking-up-your-data), while the other participants described their datasets and found meaningful potential links. [![2014-04-02 11.36.01](/assets/img/2014-04-02-11.36.01-e1403014649420-168x300.jpg)](/assets/img/2014-04-02-11.36.01.jpg)

At the end of the datathon, the following sets of links where produced:

- 5000+ links from people in the [BiographyNet RDF data](http://eculture2.cs.vu.nl:1349) (+75K famous historical Dutch people) to people in the [Rijksmuseum collection RDF data](http://semanticweb.cs.vu.nl/clustersearch/sparql/)
- 2 links from [Dutch Ships and Sailors](http://purl.org/collections/nl/dss/vocopv/opvarenden-344716) (historical ships and sailors in the Netherlands) to [Rijksmuseum collections](http://semanticweb.cs.vu.nl/clustersearch/sparql/)
- 61 links from [Dutch Ships and Sailors](https://github.com/biktorrr/dss) Ranks to [CEDAR](http://www.cedar-project.nl/) (Dutch historical censuses as RDF Data Cube) [HISCO](http://historyofwork.iisg.nl/) (Historical International Standard Classification of Occupations) URIs
- 1320 links of [CEDAR](http://www.cedar-project.nl/) municipalities to [gemeentegeschiedenis.nl](http://www.gemeentegeschiedenis.nl/) municipalities (data-hub of historical data on Dutch municipalities)
- 33 links of [ICONCLASS](http://iconclass.org/) (classification system for cultural content) to [HISCO](http://historyofwork.iisg.nl/) occupations

Obviously, the last two sets are mostly interesting for enriching CEDAR, and they are available [here](https://www.dropbox.com/sh/o7rsrlm1ovepyzc/AACWJYcaFco7Ij0hn4t7pVp3a).

You can read more in the blogs of [Victor de Boer](http://videbo.wordpress.com/2014/06/17/vu-e-history-datathon-links-up-multiple-datasets/) and [Ivo Zandhuis](http://www.hicsuntleones.nl/dagje-data-linken-aan-de-vu/).
